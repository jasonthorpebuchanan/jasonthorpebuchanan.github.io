<!-- SHORT BIO 544 words updated Nov. 1 2026 -->
Jason Thorpe Buchanan is a tri-continentally active composer, intermedia artist, and technologist. His works explore multiplicity, causality, behavior, and the integration of live performance with emerging technologies. He is Professor für Komposition mit digitalen Medien at the Hochschule für Musik Würzburg, Germany, and leads the graduate program in Komposition mit Neuen Medien as director of the Studio für experimentelle elektronische Musik.

<!-- new para -->
Buchanan’s work has received international recognition and support from the Fulbright Foundation (HfMT Hamburg), the American-Scandinavian Foundation, EMPAC, Royaumont, TIME SPANS, the Mizzou International Composers Festival, TRANSIT Festival (Belgium), the SinusTon Festival (DE), and MATA; as recipient of two ASCAP Morton Gould Young Composer Awards, an Aaron Copland Bogliasco Fellowship (IT), as winner of Iron Composer, the Howard Hanson Orchestral Prize, and more, including selection as Artist-in-Residence at USF Verftet (Norway), the Embassy of Foreign Artists (CH), Künstlerhof Schreyahn (DE), the SWR Experimentalstudio (DE), and nomination for the 2015 Gaudeamus Prize.

<!-- new para -->
Commissions and performances have included collaborations with the <a href="http://taleaensemble.org/" target="blank">Talea Ensemble</a>, <a href="http://www.ensembleinterface.com/" target="blank">Ensemble Interface</a> (DE), <a href="http://www.ensemblenikel.com/" target="blank">Ensemble Nikel</a> (Israel), <a href="www.alarmwillsound.com" target="blank">Alarm Will Sound</a>, EXAUDI Vocal Ensemble (UK), Slagwerk Den Haag (NL), Eklekto Percussion (CH), the <a href="http://www.esm.rochester.edu/ensembles/musicanova/" target="blank">Eastman Musica Nova Ensemble</a>, TACETi (Thailand), <a href="http://www.ensemble-linea.com/" target="blank">Linea</a> (FR), 
Insomnio (NL), the <a href="http://www.mivosquartet.com/" target="blank">Mivos Quartet</a> (USA), <a href="http://www.iktuspercussion.com" target="blank">Iktus Percussion</a>, <a href="http://wildup.la" target="blank">wild Up</a>, the Blue Water Chamber Orchestra, Académie Voix Nouvelles Ensemble (FR), New European Ensemble, the Hong Kong New Music Ensemble, Line Upon Line, and more. Scenes from his multimedia opera <em>Hunger</em> have received performances at Darmstadt, <a href="http://theindustryla.org" target="blank">The Industry</a>'s FIRST TAKE in L.A., and <a href="http://matafestival.org/mata-interval/" target="blank">MATA</a> (NYC) with <a href="http://www.switchensemble.com" target="blank">the [Switch~ Ensemble]</a>. “<em>Hunger</em> is a kind of training session in mental disintegration... An ungodly opera needs ugly music, singers who produce primal sounds, an electric guitar that sounds scraped raw, a wailing orchestra effects, cuts the ear like a knife. Buchanan delivers.” – <em>L.A. Times</em>

<!-- new para -->
Jason has served as Artistic Director of <a href="http://www.switchensemble.com" target="blank"> the [Switch~ Ensemble]</a> since 2012, as well as Faculty Artist of the IntAct Festival's <a href="http://www.tmaomusic.com/" target="blank">International Composition Institute of Thailand</a> since it began in 2019. Buchanan has held roles in the <a href="https://www.hfmdd.de/en/college/institutes-facilities/hybrid-music-lab" target="blank">HfM Dresden’s Hybrid Music Lab</a> (DE), as Managing Director of the <a href="https://newmusic.missouri.edu/Mizzou" target="blank">New Music Initiative at the University of Missouri</a> (USA), as Visiting Lecturer in Composition and Interim Director of the <a href="https://music.utexas.edu/" target="blank">UT Austin Electronic Music Studios</a>, as Department Chair/Lecturer in Composition, Theory, and Electroacoustic Music at Mahidol University (Thailand), as Executive Director of the VIPA Festival (Spain), and as Curator for the San Francisco Center for New Music. In 2026, he served as Senior Composer/Conductor at the June in Buffalo Festival, leading the Slee Sinfonietta with soloist T.J. Borden in the American premiere of his own cello concerto, Like a Scattering from a Fixed Point, for digitally-augmented cellist, conductor, and ensemble.

<!-- new para -->
Buchanan holds a Ph.D. from the Eastman School of Music and is widely sought as a collaborator and guest composer by institutions such as the University of Chicago, Stanford University, the Grieg Academy (Norway), MATA (NYC), YST Conservatory (Singapore), UCSD, UC Santa Barbara, the SinusTon Festival (DE), Carnegie Mellon, TIME SPANS Festival (NYC), University of Louisville, the Juilliard School, TRANSIT Festival (Belgium), the Bergen Center for Elektronisk Kunst (Norway), and dozens of universities throughout the U.S., Europe, and Asia.
